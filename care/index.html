Amatra Care Privacy

Say it once after the visit. The paperwork is done.

Amatra Care is a voice assistant for geriatric care managers. You tap Talk and describe a visit or a call in your own words. Amatra Care drafts the visit note, the update for the family, the follow-up tasks, the appointments for your calendar and the billing for your invoice, and all of it waits for your approval.

What Amatra Care does

Visit notes

Your words become a clear, third-person note in the client's record, ready for you to review and approve.

Family updates

A warm, professional update to the family members who should hear from you, drafted from what you said and sent only when you approve it.

Tasks and appointments

Every follow-up you mention becomes a task, and every appointment goes onto your calendar with the client's name and the place.

Time and invoices

Visits, calls, travel, mileage and expenses are priced with your own rates and rounding, and each month becomes a professional invoice you can email in one tap.

A visit timer

Start the timer when you arrive and stop it when you leave, so the time you bill is the time you spent.

Your week at a glance

Today's visits, the week ahead and every open task across your clients are in one place when you open the app.

Nothing goes out without you

Amatra Care never sends an email, books an appointment or bills a family on its own. Everything it drafts waits on a review screen, where you can change any word, remove anything you do not want, and then approve it.

If you connect your Google account, approved family updates and invoices are sent from your own Gmail address, so replies come straight back to you. Approved appointments are added to your Google Calendar, and emailed invoices are filed in your Google Drive under a folder for each client. Amatra Care can send email but cannot read your inbox, and it can only see the Drive files that it creates. You can disconnect Google at any time from the app's settings.
