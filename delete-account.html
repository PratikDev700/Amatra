Delete Your Account

Amatra - Elder Care

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your Amatra account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

1
Send us an email

Email support@amatra.app from the email address associated with your Amatra account. Use the subject line "Delete My Account".

2
We verify your identity

We'll confirm your identity by verifying your email address matches an active Amatra account.

3
Your data is deleted

Within 30 days of your request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated data.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials (email and password)
  • Parent profiles (names, phone numbers, call schedules)
  • All call summaries and history
  • Mood, medication, and wellness trend data
  • Doctor and pharmacy information
  • Family member and care circle connections

What happens to scheduled calls

All scheduled daily calls for your parents will be immediately cancelled upon account deletion. Your parents will no longer receive check-in calls from Amatra.

Questions?

If you have any questions about the deletion process, reach out to us at support@amatra.app.
