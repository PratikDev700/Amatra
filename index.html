Nothing goes out without you.

Amatra drafts. You decide. Every note, update, appointment and invoice waits on one review screen, where you can change any word before you approve it.

  • It never records your visits.

    Amatra hears only what you choose to tell it after a visit or a call.

  • Recordings don’t linger.

    Once you approve or discard what was drafted from a recording, the recording is deleted.

  • Your updates come from you.

    When you connect Google, approved family updates and invoices go out from your own Gmail address, so replies come straight back to you.

If you connect your Google account, approved family updates and invoices are sent from your own Gmail address, approved appointments are added to your Google Calendar, and emailed invoices are filed in your Google Drive under a folder for each client. Amatra Care can send email but cannot read your inbox, and it can only see the Drive files that it creates. You can disconnect Google at any time from the app’s settings. Read the privacy policy.

Amatra Care is in early testing. During testing, we work with sample information rather than real client records.

Built for the parking lot, not the desk.

The work gets captured where it happens: between visits, in the car, on the way to the next appointment.
