A customer whose air conditioning has quit is not going to leave a voicemail and wait. They are going to hang up and call the next company on the list. By the time you are back in the truck with a free hand, that job belongs to somebody else.

Switchboard answers those calls instead. It finds out what is wrong, takes down the details your team needs to schedule the work, and tells the customer that somebody will call them back. You get the lead in writing before you have finished the job you were on.

How it works

1

The customer calls your number

You keep the number that is already on your trucks and your website. You decide when calls come to us, whether that is only when nobody picks up, or every evening and weekend.

2

Switchboard answers as your company

It greets callers in your company's name and sounds like somebody who has worked your front desk for years.

3

It finds out what they need

Name, callback number, service address, what the system is doing, when it started, and when they can be home. It asks the way a person would, not like a form being read out loud.

4

Your team gets the lead immediately

A written summary arrives within seconds of the call ending, by email and on your lead board, with a recording of the conversation attached to both.

5

Your team decides what happens next

Switchboard never books a job, never promises a time, and never tells anyone a technician is on the way. Scheduling stays entirely with you.

Every lead reaches you in two places.

Most of the time the email is all you need, because it arrives while you are still on the job and tells you who to call back. The board is there for the end of the week, when you want to see everything that came in and what has already been handled.

In your inbox, within seconds

One email per call, short enough to read at a traffic light. Everyone on your team who should see leads can be on it.

What you get

Answered around the clock

Nights, weekends and holidays included. The phone gets picked up whether or not anyone is at the desk.

Conversations built for HVAC

It knows the difference between a system that has stopped completely and one that is running but not keeping up, and it asks.

Leads sorted by urgency

A no-cooling call in August and a routine maintenance question do not arrive looking the same.

Written summaries

Every call arrives as a short summary your team can act on without listening to anything.

Safety calls handled first

If a caller mentions gas, carbon monoxide, smoke or sparking, they are told to get out and call 911 before anything else happens.

Escalation rules you set

You decide what counts as urgent for your business and who hears about it first.

What it does not do

Switchboard does not replace your technicians, and it is not trying to. It does not diagnose equipment, it does not tell anybody what is wrong with their system, and it never advises a customer to open a panel or touch a breaker.

It handles the first phone conversation and gets the information to the people who do the work.
